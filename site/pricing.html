Questions

Billing questions

Is there a contract or can I pay month to month?

All plans are month to month with no long-term contract. Annual billing is available at a discount.

What happens if I add more technicians mid-month?

You are billed a prorated amount for the rest of the current cycle, then the new seat count applies going forward.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no card required.
